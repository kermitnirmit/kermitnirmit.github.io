Queens Solver

Screenshot in, solved board out — for LinkedIn's daily Queens puzzle.

PythonOpenCVBacktracking

LinkedIn's Queens puzzle asks you to place one queen in every row, column and colored region without any two touching. Instead of solving it by hand, this reads a screenshot of the board and solves it for you.
